## 👋 About Me

Hi, I'm **Lee Jeongyun**. I study **ITM at SeoulTech** and **Northumbria University**, and I work as an undergraduate research intern at **BIGBASE, SeoulTech**.

I like problems where a research idea has to survive contact with a real service — a model that must keep working after deployment, a pipeline that must not silently drop data, a metric someone will actually make a decision with.
